# Schools of Magic
This document will maintain our ongoing list of schools of magic and provide high level overviews for each one. More details, such as individual techs within each and interactions with other schools, will be added to each school's individual files.
### Spatial Magic
The primary school for transportation and space management throughout the factory. This school will allow the user to create magic conduits for transporting items throughout the factory. Sometime mid game, the user will unlock the ability to create spatial rooms which allow them to deal with the limited factory space. Eventually, they will unlock portals to instantly transport items or creatures across the factory at a high mana cost to maintain them.
### Natural Magic
I am thinking this would be the primary "power" system of the game designed around harvesting mana from various natural sources in the world. I'm also open to expanding this to simultaneously be a production branch of magic for items which come from plants and creatures.
### Alchemic Magic
This would be the game's primary production school. Designed around deconstructing raw resources into various magical essences and then reconstructed into other products.
### Augmentation Magic
A school of magic designed around altering the properties/statistics of the player, creatures, or machines. These property changes would largely be tradeoffs. While you could permanently increase the throughput of a machine, it may come at the cost of mana usage or increased recipe costs.

Augmentation magic would also allow the user to create golems which could perform various tasks similar to factorio bots. Additionally, golems could be another method of securing resources from dungeons.
### Potions
Potions would largely be an alternative to the augmentation magic's attribute system. Potions would provide a raw buff to players, creatures, or machines without the tradeoffs that augmentation magic require. However, this requires a steady production of said potion. These would be fluids, and the recipe would be base + ingredients + catalyst.
### Divination Magic
A school about gathering and utilizing information. This would start as various QOF improvements to the player such as easily viewable production statistics, NPC schedules, NPC request rewards, etc. Eventually, it would open the player to a Factorio style circuit network which allows machines to output and react to information. Additionally, since resources in dungeons are limited, it would reveal to the player additional avenues to get resources. Lastly, in the late game, it would also contain the infinite researches.
### Elemental Magic?
I'm not sure about this school tbh. The thought process is that it would be centered around generating and using natural elements in higher level production chains that would require more than just raw ingredients like early game alchemic and potion recipes. It could also provide the player with more mana generation avenues idk.
### Conjuration Magic
A hidden school unlocked through quests designed around temporary constructs. Temporary resources could be conjured to deal with limited resources on the map. They would decay over time (no spoilage!), but as long as they are consumed before they dissipate, they would behave like normal resources. Could also conjure temporary familiars which could be used for offense, defense, or support.
### Chronomancy
Another unlockable school. It would be a relatively small but powerful research tree. It would allow the player to speed/slow various parts of the factory with exponentially increasing mana costs. It could also pair well with the temporary constructs of conjuration magic
### Necromancy
This would be a secret school unlocked through an NPC. It would center around restructuring the factory to operate on blood instead of mana. Blood would be generated by slaughtering animals or magical creatures.
