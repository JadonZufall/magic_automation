# High Level Overview
A factory game themed around various schools of magic, each having their own production chains, strengths, and weaknesses.

The world would be limited in size, but more fleshed out, similar to Stardew valley. This would allow the player more gameplay opportunities during times they are idling while their factory operates. 

The NPCs of the world could also be directly tied to progression. Major milestones could be locked behind "deliverables" to these characters. For example, the blacksmith might need x amount of iron per y minutes. As a reward he might grant you a magical forge which will help with the automation of certain materials moving forward. As you progress through the milestones, not only would you unlock more tools to expand the factory, but the town itself would expand, opening more opportunities for you. Unlocks might not just be an expansion of an existing school of magic, but could be entirely new schools as well.

Initially, the player would need to work around the limited space, but eventually they would unlock new avenues to workaround these such as spatial rooms.
